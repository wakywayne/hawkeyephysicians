About Hawkeye

We built what we
wished existed.
A teleradiology
company that works.

Hawkeye Physicians PLLC was founded on a straightforward observation: legacy teleradiology companies weren't meeting the needs of the hospitals they serve nor the radiologists who service them. They are too expensive and turn around time SLAs just stop being met.

We set out to build a different kind of teleradiology company — one structured around the specific clients we serve - the hospitals and patients who need our help when they are most exposed and the radiologists who service them, backed by leadership you can actually reach when something needs attention.

That's still what we do. And when you call us, you reach the people who built it.

What We Believe

Coverage gaps are an emergency.

When a hospital doesn't have radiology coverage, patients wait for reads, clinicians make decisions without imaging, and liability accumulates. We treat that as the emergency it is — not a scheduling inconvenience.

Accountability is personal.

When you call us, you get leadership directly. When something needs attention — a missed SLA, a credentialing question, a coverage concern — you reach Hawkeye leadership directly. That's not a feature we added. It's how we built the company, and it's what we mean when we say you can trust us with your patients.
