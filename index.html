Common Questions

Questions we hear before every partnership.

Straight answers to the things hospital leaders need to know before they're ready to talk.

How quickly can we actually go live?
Most facilities can go live in approximately 6 weeks. Our integration team handles the PACS connection, credentialing verification, and workflow configuration. Your technologists upload the same way they always have.
Why not just hire additional in-house radiologists?
Hiring a dedicated team is challenging, from both staffing and costs perspectives. We can provide a large team where you only pay for what you use. Many members of our team are hungry to jump on if there's a surge. TATs never get unmanageable.
Who is actually reading our cases?
All reads are performed by U.S. -trained, U.S.-based, ABR / AOBR board-certified radiologists.
How does credentialing work?
Our team of in-house credentialers prepares and submits the applications for our radiologists to your hospital clients' Medical Staff Offices.
How reliable is our service?
No one from our client practices has ever had to read a case while we were on duty.
