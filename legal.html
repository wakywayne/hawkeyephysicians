Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 10, 2026

1Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Hawkeye Physicians PLLC ("Hawkeye," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website (the "Site"). It applies to visitors browsing the Site and to individuals who submit information through our contact form or other inquiry channels on the Site.

Note: This policy covers website visitor data only. It does not govern protected health information (PHI) handled through Hawkeye's clinical teleradiology services — see Section 9 for how that distinction works.

2Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — when you submit our contact form, this may include your name, work email address, organization, role, and any details you choose to share about your inquiry.
  • Information collected automatically — when you visit the Site, we may automatically collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and referring URL, typically through cookies or similar technologies. See Section 5.
  • Information from third parties — No third party information is collected.

We do not collect protected health information (PHI) or any patient data through this Site.

3How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form and follow up regarding our services
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site's functionality and content
  • Understand how visitors use the Site through aggregated, anonymized analytics
  • Communicate with you about our services, where you have provided consent to do so
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraudulent or unauthorized activity

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use information submitted through this Site for advertising targeting purposes.

4How We Share Information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who perform functions on our behalf, such as website hosting, email delivery, and analytics — bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations
  • Legal and compliance purposes, where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
  • Business transfers, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to standard confidentiality protections

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5Cookies & Analytics

The Site may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to support core functionality and to understand aggregate visitor behavior.

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain Site features.

6Data Retention

We retain information submitted through the contact form for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records.

7Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you, which may include the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict our use of your information, and the right to opt out of certain data uses.

To exercise any applicable rights, contact us using the information in Section 11.

8Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information submitted through the Site from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9Relationship to HIPAA

Hawkeye's clinical teleradiology services involve the handling of protected health information (PHI) and are subject to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and related regulations. That clinical data handling is governed by separate agreements with our healthcare facility partners, not by this Site's Privacy Policy.

This Site itself is a marketing and informational website. It is not used to transmit, store, or process PHI, and information submitted through the contact form (such as your name and work email) does not constitute PHI under HIPAA.

10Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for business and healthcare professional audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16, where applicable under local law). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
