How We're Built Differently

A direct model, not a layered one.

Most teleradiology runs through a chain of intermediaries that adds cost and distance between you and the radiologist reading your case.

Built On Modern Tools

AI-assisted, cloud-based, location-free.

Our radiologists aren't tied to a single workstation behind hospital walls. Cloud-based viewing and AI-assisted reading tools let subspecialists work securely from wherever they're scheduled.

What It Means For You

Less overhead, more reliability.

Fewer intermediaries and modern infrastructure translate into a model your organization can actually run on: predictable coverage, straightforward contracting, and a partner that's structured to scale with you rather than around you.

The Platform

Connects to what you already run.

  • HL7 FHIR connectivity for orders, results, and reporting workflows
  • Cloud-based diagnostic viewer — no install required on your end
